# How does your school calculate and report marks?

Hey, I need your help with something. I’m building School Portal so teachers can capture marks, calculate results and issue reports. I’d like to check that a learner’s marks in the portal would come out the same as they do on your school’s mark sheet.

Grade 10 Mathematics is the example I’m working on. If you teach another grade or subject, I’d be happy to hear how yours works. Please mention your **grade, subject, province and the year your answers apply to**. If your school is still waiting for its 2027 programme, the most recent year you’ve taught is helpful.

You can reply by question number. If you only have time for one part, the worked example in question 21 would help me most. Please use made-up marks and leave out learner names, admission numbers and personal details.

## 1. SBA and assessments

1. Which tasks normally count towards School-Based Assessment (SBA) in your subject? Where do tests, assignments, projects, investigations and exams fit in?
2. Which parts of that structure are common across subjects or grades, and which are specific to yours?
3. Where do you get each task’s weight? Which parts are prescribed by the DBE or province, and which can your school decide?
4. Can the same task count differently towards a term mark and the annual SBA? An example of a task that counts towards one but carries less or no weight in the other would help.
5. How do you hear about changes each year? If you have the programme or circular your school follows, could you share its title, date and section or a public link? Has your school received anything that confirms which rules apply in 2027?

## 2. Calculating marks

6. Could you show how you calculate one term mark using two or three tasks with different maximum marks? Do you add the raw marks first, or work out a percentage for each task and then apply its weight?
7. How do you calculate the final SBA percentage? Do you start with the individual tasks, category totals or the term marks already printed on reports?
8. How do the SBA and the final exam combine to give the annual subject mark? Does that split vary by grade or subject?
9. How do you combine Paper 1 and Paper 2? If they have different totals, or a learner misses one paper, what goes on the mark sheet?
10. At which stages do you round, and to how many decimal places? For example, if the underlying result is **79.495%**, what would the teacher see, what would appear on the report, and which achievement level would you assign? Does the level come from the underlying value or the printed percentage?

## 3. Missing marks and absences

11. If a learner misses a test with a valid reason, what do you enter while waiting? If they write a replacement, who authorises it and which mark counts?
12. What do you enter for an absence without a valid reason? Who decides the final treatment, and how can a teacher tell it apart from a learner who wrote the task and scored zero?
13. If a learner is formally exempt, who authorises the exemption and how do you handle that task’s weight? A small example showing the calculation after the exemption would help.
14. If a mark is still missing when reports are due, what happens to the subject result and to the rest of the report? What does the family see, and who resolves it?

## 4. Reviewing and publishing marks

15. Who checks the captured marks before they become official? What does the teacher, head of department (HOD) or administrator check against the recording sheet?
16. Who approves the SBA marks? Can the person who captured them also approve them? Who gives the final go-ahead to release results to families?
17. If you find an error after a report has gone out, who requests and approves the correction? What can the family see while it is being checked, and how do they recognise the revised report?
18. Can learners and parents see individual task marks during the term? If so, at what point can they see them, and which comments or reasons stay with staff?

## 5. Reports and practical examples

19. What has to appear on a term report, and who approves the complete report? Which comments, achievement descriptions or signatures are required? Who decides promotion across subjects at year-end?
20. Could you share a blank recording sheet, report template or anonymised example that you’re allowed to share? A blank copy with the column headings and formulas intact would be ideal.
